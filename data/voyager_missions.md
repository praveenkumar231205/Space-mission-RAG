# Voyager Missions

Voyager 1 and Voyager 2 are twin NASA spacecraft launched in 1977 to explore the outer planets. Voyager 2 launched first, on August 20, 1977, and Voyager 1 followed on September 5, 1977. They took advantage of a rare alignment of the outer planets that occurs about once every 175 years.

## Planetary encounters

Voyager 1 flew past Jupiter in 1979 and Saturn in 1980. Voyager 2 visited Jupiter in 1979, Saturn in 1981, Uranus in January 1986 and Neptune in August 1989. Voyager 2 remains the only spacecraft ever to have visited Uranus and Neptune.

## Interstellar space

Voyager 1 crossed the heliopause, the boundary of the Sun's influence, and entered interstellar space in August 2012. Voyager 2 did the same in November 2018. Voyager 1 is the most distant human-made object, more than 24 billion kilometers from Earth.

## Golden Record

Each spacecraft carries a Golden Record, a gold-plated copper disc containing sounds, images and greetings from Earth, intended for any intelligent life that might find it. Both probes are powered by radioisotope thermoelectric generators, whose output declines over time. Engineers have been switching off instruments one by one to extend the missions.
