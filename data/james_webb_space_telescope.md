# James Webb Space Telescope

The James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) is the largest and most powerful space telescope ever launched. It is a joint project of NASA, the European Space Agency and the Canadian Space Agency.

## Launch and orbit

JWST launched on December 25, 2021 on an Ariane 5 rocket from Kourou, French Guiana. It travels in a halo orbit around the Sun-Earth Lagrange point L2, about 1.5 million kilometers from Earth, on the side facing away from the Sun.

## Design

Webb observes mainly in infrared light, which lets it see through dust clouds and detect very distant, redshifted galaxies. Its primary mirror is 6.5 meters wide and is made of 18 hexagonal segments of gold-coated beryllium. That is much larger than the 2.4-meter mirror of the Hubble Space Telescope, which launched in 1990. A sunshield made of five layers, about the size of a tennis court, keeps the instruments cold, below about minus 223 degrees Celsius.

## Science

The first full-color images and spectra were released on July 12, 2022. Webb studies the first galaxies that formed after the Big Bang, the birth of stars and planetary systems, and the atmospheres of exoplanets.
