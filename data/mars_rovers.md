# Mars Rovers

Rovers are wheeled robotic explorers that drive across the surface of Mars to study its geology and search for signs of past life.

## Early rovers

Sojourner, a small rover that arrived with the Mars Pathfinder lander in 1997, was the first rover to operate on Mars. The twin rovers Spirit and Opportunity landed in January 2004. Opportunity far outlasted its planned 90-day mission. Contact was lost in June 2018 during a global dust storm, and NASA declared the mission complete in February 2019.

## Curiosity

Curiosity landed on August 6, 2012 inside Gale Crater using a rocket-powered "sky crane" maneuver. It is powered by a radioisotope power system rather than solar panels. Curiosity found evidence that Gale Crater once held a lake with chemistry suitable for microbial life.

## Perseverance and Ingenuity

Perseverance landed on February 18, 2021 in Jezero Crater, a former river delta. It collects and seals rock core samples that a future mission could bring back to Earth. Its MOXIE experiment demonstrated that oxygen can be produced from the carbon dioxide in Mars's atmosphere.

Perseverance carried the small helicopter Ingenuity, which made the first powered, controlled flight on another planet on April 19, 2021. Ingenuity flew 72 times before its mission ended in January 2024 after rotor damage.

## Other missions

China's Zhurong rover landed in Utopia Planitia in May 2021 as part of the Tianwen-1 mission.
