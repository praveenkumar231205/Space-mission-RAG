# Artemis Program

Artemis is NASA's program to return humans to the Moon and to prepare for later missions to Mars. It is named after Artemis, the twin sister of Apollo in Greek mythology. The program uses the Space Launch System (SLS) rocket and the Orion crew capsule. Orion's European Service Module is provided by the European Space Agency.

## Missions

Artemis I was an uncrewed test flight. It launched on November 16, 2022, sent Orion around the Moon, and splashed down in the Pacific Ocean on December 11, 2022.

Artemis II is the first crewed mission of the program, a flight around the Moon without landing. Its crew is Reid Wiseman, Victor Glover, Christina Koch and Jeremy Hansen of the Canadian Space Agency. Hansen is the first non-American assigned to a lunar mission.

Artemis III is planned to land astronauts near the lunar south pole, an area believed to contain water ice in permanently shadowed craters. NASA selected SpaceX's Starship as the Human Landing System for the landing. Schedules for later Artemis missions have changed several times, so check NASA for current dates.

## Artemis Accords

The Artemis Accords are a set of non-binding principles for peaceful and transparent cooperation in space exploration, first signed in 2020. India signed the Accords in June 2023.
